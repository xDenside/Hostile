All sounds in this folder are released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0):
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
They were trimmed, level-matched and encoded to mono MP3 here. "Slowed" means the
recording is played slower, which also lowers it, by the factor shown.
